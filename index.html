Welcome!

CitiBike is North America's largest bike share system. Thousands of stations serve tens of thousands of bikes, which serve millions of riders across four boroughs with ridership on the rise.

However, there are still large areas of the city that are not served by CitiBike.

This map uses bike trip data, land use density, and bike route information to show which neighborhoods are primed for the next round of expansion!

Explore the map to see CitiBike stations, bike routes, land use density, and bike trip demand across the city.

Use the checkboxes to toggle different layers on the map and gain insights into bike share usage and urban density.
